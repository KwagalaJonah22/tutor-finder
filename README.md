# TutorConnect - Student Tutor Finder Platform
 📌 Project Overview

TutorConnect. is a web platform that connects students with qualified tutors across Uganda. It solves the real-world problem of students struggling to find reliable tutors for challenging subjects.

 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students in Ugandan universities and high schools struggle to find qualified tutors for subjects they find challenging. Traditional methods like asking friends, checking notice boards, or searching WhatsApp groups are:
  Slow- Takes days or weeks to find a tutor
  Unreliable- No verification of tutor qualifications  
  Expensive - No price transparency or comparison

 ✅ Solution

TutorConnect provides a simple, fast, and reliable platform where students can:
- Browse qualified tutors by subject
- Filter by subject category
- View tutor ratings and experience
- Contact tutors directly

 👥 Target Users

- **Students** - University and high school students needing academic support
- **Tutors** - Qualified educators looking for students
